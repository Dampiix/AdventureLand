Gear Progression Guide

A compact reading of the community gear chart from the Adventure Land Discord. Every icon was matched pixel by pixel against the game's item sprites (game data v18285), so names and sources are exact; upgrade levels are read from the chart.

Stages

Start – first full set: shop armor and Fire weapons.
Mid – event, boss and crafted pieces.
Late – the same ideas pushed to +9/+10.

Single vs. AOE

Damage classes show two setups: Single for bosses and lone targets, AOE for packs. Where both agree, the cell spans both columns.

Reading a cell

Icon with upgrade level (the chart shows +10 as X and +5 as V) and ×2 for a pair of rings or earrings. A coloured frame marks a new item compared with the stage before.

Common ground for every class

  • Start: shop Helmet, Coat, Pants, Shoes and Gloves +6 (priest: Wanderer's set +5); main-stat earrings +1, amulet +2, rings +2, belt +1; Cape +4, Loaded Die orb, Pumpkin Spice Latte.
  • Mid: Winged Boots +9, Super Mittens +5, Fluffy Blanket +7, Coat and Pants +9, Loaded Die +2, main-stat belt +4. Physical classes take Band of Fury and Sucker Punch rings, casters Rings of the Crypt.
  • Late: armor and weapons +10, Winged Boots +10, accessories +5, Loaded Die +3. Warrior and rogue switch to Pants of the Fallen Master; paladin, priest and rogue to Mana Gloves.
