Gear Plan v2.0

General gear progression rules the bot follows. Derived from the community chart and our own decisions. The bot builds its class wishes from exactly this plan (bot/config.js, gearPlan).

How the bot uses it

  1. Every slot has a ladder: base -> better pieces -> end goal. Each step has a target upgrade level.
  2. Keep upgrading instead of waiting: the worn piece goes up to its cap until the next step is in hand.
  3. The game's progression valuation decides what is better (the bot's equip pass already uses it). The plan only says what to wish for and what stands in.
  4. A missing item is replaced by its stand-in (e.g. green Lolipop Mace for the orange one).
  5. Wish: shop, craft and farmable drops are pursued actively. Found: event and boss items are worn when we have them, never chased.
  6. Item stages: an item goes up level by level across the team - e.g. STR rings +4 are wished only once every STR-ring wish at +3 is fulfilled. Different items never block each other.
  7. A worn alternative pauses the ladder of its slot: while e.g. a Loaded Die is worn, no class orb is built.
  8. Shop weapons stop at +8 and only while no better copy is there (a worn Fiery Blade +7 already beats a Blade +8: 54 + burn vs 50). Above that the fire weapon ladder is the way up.
  9. Wanderer's set: each piece has the stats of the shop piece of its slot plus a little (cap +120 HP, attire +80 MP, breeches +1 speed) and the set bonus (2: +200 HP, 3: +100 MP, 4: +10% gold, 5: +16 luck). Only the chest opener (priest) runs it (2026-11-23): it wishes its pieces actively (2026-11-20); once its Wanderer goals are done, spare copies are sold. The mage carries a +0 set as its luck swap set (2026-11-26).

Ladder

Left to right. The bot wishes for the next step and upgrades what it wears up to the step's level; an earlier step is the stand-in for a later one.

Alternative

A different item for the same slot, worn when the game's valuation rates it higher. While one is worn, the ladder of its slot pauses.

When found

Event, boss and box items. Worn when we have them and they rate higher – never farmed or bought on purpose.

Decisions

  • 2026-11-19: Elixirs: not for now. · Dagger of the Dead may be crafted from Bones for the rogue; it joins the weapon ladder. · Jewelry (rings, earrings, amulets, belts) to +4 with item stages. · Wanderer's set runs in parallel with the shop armor (drops only, no spare copies kept). · No risk floors and no grace preparation. · The merchant works on wishes by value (gain per gold / per hour). · Winged Boots built to +7 (rare scrolls from +8). · The warrior stays on Fiery Blades (no Swords of the Dead). · Shop weapons run through the stages with the armor: everything to +7 first, then +8.
  • 2026-11-20: The fighter with the highest gold bonus opens the party's chests (the opener's gold multiplier counts for the whole chest). · The priest (our opener) wishes Wanderer's Attire, Breeches and Gloves +8 once its shop gear is done - with the cap +10% gold. Bases from Ponty; never a stat loss (delivered only at +8). · Wanderer's Shoes +8 stand in for the Winged Boots on the priest (full set: +16 luck); finished Winged Boots replace them. · Shop gear and armor take the High scroll from +5 -> +6 on. · Stages are team-wide: everyone's shop gear to +7, then everyone to +8. · Weapon wishes and Ponty finds only for the weapon types of the class's plan.
  • 2026-11-23: Wanderer's set only for the priest (chest opener, luck catch); spare copies are sold once its goals are done. · Rugged +0 copies nobody wears and nobody wishes are sold (e.g. Rugged Coats after the warrior's Dracul's Attire).
  • 2026-11-26: The mage carries a +0 Wanderer's set as its luck swap set (the +16 luck is the five-piece set bonus; the level adds none). · Gear plan split: `wishes` for the class's own strength, `bonus` for extras (the luck swap sets). · Predefined sets (SETS, setItems, setWishes at the top of config.js) - the Wanderer's set first; later e.g. a default kit for every class.
