Cave of Many Dreams - the 50 encounters

Every vote the cave can put to us. The options are sorted by their value in Amber, best first - only 2 of 6 are offered per vote, so take the first one offered. BEST = the top option, NEVER = never take it. Effects marked "unverified" / "est" come from the guide and the option text.

Rules that matter

  • 3 characters, 3 floors, 24 minutes; forced votes pause the clock (1 minute to vote, a majority ends it early). One visit per account per day.
  • Each floor: guard camp (3 packs; each cleared pack: a camp roll + a chest with 1,500 cave gold + 1 Amber), floor keeper (a BOSS roll + chest: 4,000 cave gold), marked encounter - all three open the stairs. Floor 3 done: a finish roll + 10,000 cave gold + 5 Amber.
  • A "roll" (parcel, rescue, BOSS, camp, finish) is one draw from that table right away - not an item to keep. Items go to a random member of the original party (bag, or MAIL when full).
  • How to use a card: the options are sorted by value, best first. Only 2 of the 6 are offered per vote - go down the list and take the first one offered. "est" = an estimate for an effect without game data.
  • Values in Amber (agreed 2026-11-24): 1,000 cave gold = 0.65 (it is lost at the end unless a shop has something useful - only the Loaded Die really counts); 1 second = -0.02 (the first run made ~1.2 Amber a minute); a regular cave monster = +0.05 (8 % Amber minus its time); a traveler or rogue fight -0.16 (+ a parcel roll if hostile, est); a keeper from an encounter -0.2; an ally +0.3, pry bar +0.2, lamp +0.1. Items: Loaded Die 10, Tunnel Axe 0.2, gem1/scroll1 0.1, Counterweight, Locktooth, Mothstep Boots, Reed Scythe ~0.
  • One roll is worth: parcel 0.96 · rescue 1.82 · BOSS 1.67 · finish 2.77 Amber.
  • Death costs nothing; revive at the floor doorway is free, in place 1 Amber. Exit leaves for good.
  • Cave gold stays in the cave - spend it (merchant near each floor's doorway, upper wall). Unspent Amber goes to the party. Limits per run: 60,000 cave gold, 36 Amber.
  • Never: the red chain / red rope (six level 100 wolves), the copying shadow, the Dark Mage (100,000 magic damage, mages first - run).
  • Monsters scale to the party level (first run, level 73): every regular cave monster had 3,665 HP, 187 attack, 110 armor, 73 resistance - wolves probably too. Travelers: 19,732 HP, 281 attack. The base values below are the game data, not what you meet.
  • Stairs: a vote ("landing") at the open stairs takes the whole party down. INFO -> Directions lists every room with a "Go" button.
  • Our bots: you vote on the priest, warrior and mage copy your vote at once.
